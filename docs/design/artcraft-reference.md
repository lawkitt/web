## Observed structure

community action. Its desktop lineup is a four-column card grid. Cards combine
category, screenshot, icon, name, pitch, maturity, installer availability,
platforms and an Explore link. At a 390px phone width the cards stack vertically.

platform downloads, source and related apps. Section anchors accompany the catalog.
